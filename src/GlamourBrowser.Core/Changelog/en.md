# What's new

## 0.7.1.0
- Wishlist: the tiles show only the wish itself, without your outfit and without the try-on.
- More render views: in the settings (Debug) up to 7 tiles can render at the same time. From 3 on, the plugin also uses the slots of the game windows (character screen, try-on, dyes …) while none of them is open. The note below the slider explains what can happen with Penumbra and with windows opened meanwhile. The default stays 2, as before.

## 0.7.0.0
- Same model: a cube with a number at the bottom left of a tile shows how many other items share its model, in other colour variants too. A click shows them all as tiles, "← Back" returns to the view before. The cube is green when you own one of them.
- If you lack an item but own the same model, its top-right corner is half gray, half green. If the same model is on your wishlist, the star is half yellow.
- Dye boxes on the tiles: dyeable items show their dyes at the bottom right. A click dyes only that one tile.
- Live view with the round buttons of the game's Try On window: "Equip / remove all" (replaces the tile mode at the top), "Keep tried-on pieces", visor, draw weapon and show weapon. A hidden weapon is now hidden on the tiles too. New installations start with everything removed.
- Item card: hovering the item icon shows the game's own tooltip. The item and set cards have a wishlist star.
- Filters, mode and slot are kept after a restart. A click on "Page x / y" jumps to a page; Page Up/Down, Home and End turn pages, Shift+mouse wheel jumps 10 pages.
- "Save as set" asks for the name and leaves you in your view.
- Fixed: a worn glamour over several slots showed no torso in the live view. Clicking a worn piece no longer resets the filters. "Discard" and "Delete folder" ask first, "Reset all" stays in the mode, and "Paused" says why.

## 0.6.0.0
- Rings: the slot bar now has "Right Ring" and "Left Ring" apart. Their tiles show the ring on that hand, and each hand has its own camera.
- Automatic camera: gloves, bracelets and rings look the same on every character, one hand from the side. Left rings and bracelets for the left arm only as the mirror image.
- New zoom of the automatic camera per slot in the settings (50–300 %).
- Undo and redo for the try-on: two buttons on the right of the row with the dye and Classes/Jobs.
- Gear sets: the chest icon in the item card (below the dyes) shows all pieces of the set as tiles, "← Back" returns to the previous view. The tiles' right-click menu no longer lists sets.
- Without a weapon in the try-on, the tiles no longer show a default weapon either.

## 0.5.0.0
- Folders for your own sets: create and name folders, put sets in by dragging or with the right-click menu. A folder's tile shows its first four sets as a collage; rename and delete it in the tile's top-left corner (its sets stay).
- Mark several of your own sets with Ctrl+click or Shift+click and move them together. While dragging, a small copy of the tile follows the cursor.
- Edit and delete your own sets right on their tile, below the wishlist star.
- Automatic camera: the tiles frame the head, hands, feet and the whole body to fit your character's race and height. Your own calibration still comes first.
- Bracelets worn on one arm only are shown from their side, and a new filter shows bracelets on the right, the left or both arms (combinable).
- The live view's camera stays where you put it; a double-click resets it.
- The job filter shows jobs only, without base classes such as Gladiator. The filter column no longer cuts off text, and the source icons are larger.
- After an update over several versions this window shows what's new in all of them.

## 0.4.1.0
- Market prices: hovering the market board icon of a tile or the item card shows the cheapest listing on your home world and on your data center (Universalis). A click in the item card opens the item on Universalis.
- Try-on: right-clicking a piece below the live view takes it off, the weapons too. Without a weapon no default weapon shows up any more, and drawing the weapon is grayed out.
- Clicking a piece below the live view jumps the tiles to its slot and to the page with the item.
- Rings: a ring tile's right-click menu puts the ring on the right or the left hand.
- New button in the item card that copies the item's name.
- Pre-rendering pages now follows the paging direction: paging back and on the last page, the pages before are prepared.

## 0.4.0.0
- The plugin now speaks your game's language: English, German, French or Japanese. You can also pick one in the settings.
- Live view: open/close the visor, draw the weapon and hide the weapon right in the view's top-left corner. The cog in the bottom-right corner sets how fast rotating, panning and zooming react.
- Live view: with idle poses 5 and 6 the character takes its battle stance again when drawing the weapon, and a drawn weapon stays drawn when you switch slots.
- Your own designs without a helmet no longer show a helmet on their tiles.
- New sort orders: level ascending, oldest first, name A–Z and name Z–A.
- Tidied up: My Sets, Wishlist, tile dye and Classes/Jobs sit in a second row under the slot selection. Tile mode, sort order and tiles per page have labels, and the filter column button sits next to "Dim".
- The icons of the worn pieces under the live view are larger. A new button in the item card jumps to the item's tile so you can browse on from there.
- When logging in, the character no longer shows up in underwear while the game is still loading the gear.
- New "Text size" setting, on top of the UI scale.
- Item card: larger buttons and icons, and the item's dyes sit large in the top-right corner.
- After an update this window shows what is new.
